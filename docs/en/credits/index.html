Made by

bubbulies is designed and developed by Rawan Alghamdi.

Artwork and sound

Bubbi the bubble mascot, the app icon and the five world illustrations are original artwork made for bubbulies. The bubbles, effects and interface graphics are drawn by the game's own code. Music and sound effects are synthesised by the game. No third-party art packs, photographs, sound recordings or font files are used.

Open-source software in the game

  • Phaser 3, the game framework (MIT License).
  • Capacitor and its App, Haptics and Preferences plugins (MIT License).
