Contact us

Email bubbulies.app@gmail.com. You can write in English or Arabic.

What to include

  • The game version (shown at the bottom of Settings), your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The level or mode, and the steps that led to the problem.
  • A screenshot, if it shows no personal information.
  • Never send passwords, verification codes or other sign-in details. We will never ask for them.

How to play

Drag above the launcher to aim, then release to shoot. Match three or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them. Bubbles that are no longer connected to the ceiling fall for extra points. Use the aim preview to plan wall bounces, and swap your current and next bubble before a shot when it helps.

Progress and new devices

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes progress that has not been saved to an online account. To keep your progress on a new device, open Profile, tap Connect account, then link Sign in with Apple. You can also save a copy of your device data from Settings → Privacy and account → Request a copy of your data.

Retries and purchases

Retries are free, and there are no lives to wait for. bubbulies has no real-money purchases, so there is nothing to buy, restore or refund.

Ad rewards

Ads are optional and need an internet connection and a connected account. After an ad, the reward can show as pending for a short while until our server confirms it; it will arrive even if you close the app. You do not need to watch the ad again. If no ad loads, try again later; normal play is never blocked.

Comfort and accessibility

In Settings you can switch music, sound effects and haptics on or off separately, reduce motion, show colour symbols on bubbles, turn the aim guide on or off, and choose English or Arabic.

Privacy and deleting data

Your privacy choices are in Settings → Privacy and account. To delete your cloud account, see the Account deletion page. To erase progress on your iPhone, use Reset progress on this device.
