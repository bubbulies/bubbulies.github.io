Who we are

bubbulies (“we”, “us”) is an iPhone game published on the App Store by Bash To Ash, the developer of bubbulies, who is responsible for the information described here. This policy covers the bubbulies app and the website bubbulies.github.io.

Questions and requests about privacy: bubbulies.app@gmail.com.

In short

  • Playing needs no account. Progress, settings and earned items are saved on your device.
  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Online features are optional and start only when you tap Connect account. They run on Google Firebase.
  • Ads are optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only.
  • Game analytics and crash reports are off by default; you decide whether to switch them on.
  • You can delete your cloud account inside the app at any time.

Information kept on your device

Most of bubbulies works entirely on your iPhone. The app saves the following in its own storage on your device. It is not sent to us unless you connect an online account, as described in the next section.

  • Level progress: stars, scores, attempts and play statistics.
  • Coins, boosters and cosmetic items earned while playing as a device guest.
  • Settings: language, music, sound effects, haptics, reduced motion, colour symbols and the aim guide.
  • Your colour theme (Classic or Ocean).
  • Your choices about game analytics and crash reports.
  • References to ad rewards that are still being confirmed, so they are not lost if the app closes.
  • If you are connected: copies of rankings, your friends list and daily challenges, kept for up to one day so the game does not download them again and again.

The theme question

When you first open the game, it can ask whether you would like a “male” or “female” style. Your answer is used once to choose a colour theme and is then discarded. Only the resulting theme (Classic or Ocean) is saved, and only on your device. The answer itself is never stored, never sent to us or anyone else, and never used for advertising or analytics. You can skip the question and change the theme at any time in Settings.

Optional online account

If you open Profile and tap Connect account, the app creates a guest account with Google Firebase Authentication. You can then link it to Sign in with Apple so your progress can be restored on another device. We do not request your name or email address from Apple.

While you are connected, the following is stored with your account on Firebase:

  • Account identifiers: a random Firebase user ID and, if you link Apple, the account identifier Apple provides for bubbulies.
  • Your nickname (2 to 20 characters) and built-in avatar, if you set them, and a random friend code.
  • For each level, only your best score and best star rating. Every other attempt, time and score stays on your device.
  • Short-lived run records used to verify level completions, prevent duplicate rewards and check daily and weekly challenges. They hold the level, stars and counters such as bubbles matched and dropped, not your score. They are deleted automatically 8 days after they are created. Unfinished level attempts (the board and shots needed to approve a booster or continue) are also deleted 8 days after their last update.
  • Your Coin and booster balance and a receipt for each reward or spend (amounts only, no gameplay detail).
  • Daily records (the free daily gift and eligible replays) and challenge rewards you have claimed.
  • Bonus Pop attempts and replays, deleted 30 days after the attempt expires. Your first-completion record and best Bonus Pop score stay with your account.
  • Ranked Timed and Endless runs: the challenge, your shots and the score our server calculates. Run evidence is kept for 90 days to review fair play. Your best ranking entries stay while your account exists.
  • Friends: your friend connections, invitations (which expire after 7 days) and the players you have blocked.
  • Ad reward records, described in the advertising section.
  • Request counters used to prevent abuse.

Rankings and friends

Rankings show the top 50 players on each board, plus your own position. Each row shows a player name and a score. The name is your nickname or an automatically generated alias such as “Player 1a2b”. Other players never see your email address or Apple ID.

Your nickname and avatar are visible to your friends and to players you send an invitation to or receive one from. Your profile is not otherwise public. bubbulies has no chat. You can remove or block a player at any time.

Optional rewarded ads

bubbulies shows only optional rewarded video ads, and only after you tap a clearly labelled button that states the reward (Coins, a booster or a continue). There are no banner ads and no forced ads, and you never have to watch an ad to play or retry. Ad rewards need a connected account, because our server confirms each reward with Google before it is added.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's IP address (used to estimate a general location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, as described in Google's privacy policy. To confirm a reward, we give Google your Firebase user ID and a one-time attempt reference; Google returns them to our server with a signed receipt.

Non-personalised ads: bubbulies asks Google for non-personalised ads only. Ads are not chosen based on your activity in other apps and websites. Google may still use information to show ads that suit the content, limit how often you see an ad, prevent fraud and measure ad performance.

Consent: before any ad loads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show the consent messages required where you live. That covers the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and US states with consumer-privacy laws. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy and account → Advertising privacy choices.

Tracking: bubbulies does not itself show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If a tracking request is ever shown for bubbulies, for example as part of Google's consent message, declining has no effect on gameplay or rewards. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Game analytics and crash reports (off by default)

Settings → Optional sharing has two switches. Both are off until you turn them on:

  • Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): play and session measurements such as first launch, session starts, play time, and which reward offers were shown or used, linked to an app-instance identifier. Google signals and ad-personalisation signals are switched off.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crash and device details, such as the device model, iOS version and what the app was doing when it crashed, to help us fix problems.
  • Turning a switch off stops new collection. Information already sent is kept by Google for the period set for our Firebase project, and it is not deleted automatically when you delete your account. Email us if you want it deleted.

Service providers

We use these providers. Their own privacy policies are linked at the end of this page.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Remote Config, Analytics and Crashlytics) runs the online features for us. While you are connected, the app downloads game settings such as active reward offers from Remote Config. Remote Config receives basic app and device information, for example the app version and language.
  • App Check uses Apple's App Attest (or DeviceCheck) to confirm that requests come from the genuine bubbulies app. It does not use your personal details.
  • Google AdMob provides the optional rewarded ads.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and App Store distribution.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

Where information is processed

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google, so online information may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google's data-processing terms include safeguards for these international transfers.

Why we use information

  • To run the game and the online features you choose to use (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To keep Coins, rewards and rankings fair and to prevent cheating and abuse (our legitimate interest in a fair, secure game).
  • To show optional ads and confirm their rewards (your consent where the law requires it; otherwise providing the reward you asked for).
  • Game analytics and crash reports (only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To answer messages you send us and to meet legal obligations.

How long information is kept

  • Device data: until you reset it, delete the app, or the app replaces it. Cached rankings, friends and challenges are refreshed after one day.
  • Cloud account data (profile, best scores, Coins, receipts, friends, rankings and claimed rewards): until you delete your account.
  • Level run records and unfinished attempts: deleted automatically 8 days after they are created or last updated.
  • Bonus Pop attempts and ranked challenges: 30 days after they expire. Ranked run evidence: 90 days.
  • After an account is deleted: a marker made of the random user ID and the deletion time, kept for 7 days so an old sign-in session cannot recreate the account.
  • Analytics and crash reports, if you turned them on: for the period set in Google's settings for our project.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to handle your request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Automatic deletion is carried out by scheduled clean-up in the database, so it can run shortly after the dates above.

Your choices and rights

You can play offline without an account, keep analytics and crash reports off, change your ad choices, export your device save (Settings → Privacy and account → Request a copy of your data), reset it (Reset progress on this device), and delete your cloud account in the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of information or targeted advertising as defined by some US state laws. To make a request, email bubbulies.app@gmail.com. We may ask for information that shows the account is yours, such as your friend code. We reply within one month, or sooner if your local law requires it. If you disagree with our answer, you can ask us to reconsider it, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

bubbulies is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are requested for a general audience, are not marked as child-directed, and are limited to content rated for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online account or sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Online information is sent over encrypted connections. The app cannot write to the database directly: only our server functions can change Coins, scores, rankings or friends, and each request is checked against your signed-in account and App Check. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

This website

bubbulies.github.io has no cookies, analytics, scripts, forms or embedded third-party content. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

When the app's use of information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Where a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or on its App Store page.
