The five worlds

The game

Drag above the launcher to aim, release to shoot, and match three or more bubbles of the same colour. Bubbles that lose their hold on the ceiling fall for extra points. Bounce shots off the walls and swap your current and next bubble when it helps.

  • 50 hand-made Adventure levels across five worlds, plus 10 quick Bonus Pop stages.
  • Free retries: no lives and no waiting.
  • Optional rewarded ads only. You never have to watch an ad to play or to retry.
  • No real-money purchases. Coins and boosters are earned by playing.
  • Comfort settings: reduced motion, colour symbols, an aim guide, and separate music, sound and haptics switches.
  • Fully playable in English and Arabic.

Privacy at a glance

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is saved on your iPhone.
  • Online features (cloud save, friends, rankings and ad rewards) start only when you choose Connect account.
  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Game analytics and crash reports are off unless you switch them on in Settings.
  • You can delete your cloud account inside the app at any time, or ask us by email.

Who it's for

bubbulies is a general-audience game for players aged 13 and over. Questions? Write to bubbulies.app@gmail.com.
