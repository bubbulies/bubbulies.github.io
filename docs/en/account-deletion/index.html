Account deletion

How to delete your bubbulies cloud account in the app or by email, what is deleted and what is kept.

Effective: 23 November 2026

On this page

  1. Delete your account in the app
  2. What is deleted
  3. What is kept
  4. Ask us by email

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open bubbulies and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete cloud account.
  3. If your account is linked to Apple, sign in with Apple again when asked. For your security, deletion needs a sign-in from the last few minutes.
  4. Tick the confirmation box, type DELETE and confirm.
  5. Deletion starts straight away. When it finishes, the app shows “Cloud account deleted”.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase account and its sign-in links. Your Sign in with Apple authorisation for bubbulies is revoked.
  • Your profile: nickname, avatar and friend code.
  • Best scores and stars, Coins, boosters, reward receipts, daily and challenge records, and Bonus Pop records.
  • Ranked runs and all your ranking entries.
  • Friend connections, invitations and blocks, on both sides.
  • Ad reward records linked to your account.

What is kept

  • Your device save. It is separate from the cloud account; erase it with Settings → Privacy and account → Reset progress on this device, or by deleting the app.
  • A deletion marker (the random user ID and the deletion time) for 7 days, so an old sign-in session cannot recreate the account.
  • Analytics and crash reports already sent, if you had turned them on. Google keeps them for the period set for our project. Email us to ask for their deletion.
  • Information Google AdMob holds about ads you watched, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Emails you sent us, for as long as needed to handle your request.

Ask us by email

If you cannot use the app, email bubbulies.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my bubbulies account”. Include your friend code if you have one (Profile → Friends). We may ask for information that shows the account is yours. We will delete the account within one month and reply to confirm.

If your account is linked to Apple, the quickest way is to reinstall bubbulies, sign in with Apple and delete the account in the app. A guest account that was never linked can only be identified from its device. If you no longer have that device, we may not be able to find the account.
