Original bubbulies vector artwork

cloud-meadows.svg, candy-skies.svg, crystal-lagoon.svg,
starlight-valley.svg, moonlight-kingdom.svg, bubble-mascot.svg,
app-icon.svg, and its app-icon-1024.png export were authored specifically
for this project.
They contain no third-party photographs, illustrations, font files,
or copied image content. The visual references informed the mood only.
